I'm a third year DPhil student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford, supervised by [Tom Rainforth](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~twgr/) and [François Caron](https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/people/francois-caron). Broadly speaking, I am interested in developing reliable statistical methods for label-efficient testing and monitoring of machine learning models. Previously, I have worked on conformal prediction [[1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.04236), [2](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=VAHHX1Trwq)], active testing/learning [[3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08347), [4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.25926)], and generative modeling [[5](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/7698d7ce4e4fc5edfe71e362cd35ac5b-Paper-Conference.pdf), [6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.27617), [7](https://openreview.net/forum?id=PoNfh0pCUe)].


Before starting my PhD, I completed my MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Imperial College London where I was awarded a [Google DeepMind Scholarship](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/prospective-students/deepmind-scholars/). For my dissertation, I analyzed the quality of Bayesian neural network approximations under the supervision of [Mark van der Wilk](https://mvdw.uk/).


I am also passionate about social mobility and widening participation in higher education. Previously, I was an [EDI fellow](https://www.mpls.ox.ac.uk/equality-diversity-and-inclusion-in-mpls/mpls-ed-i-initiatives/mpls-ed-i-fellows) at the University of Oxford, where I had the opportunity to work on the university's postgraduate recruitment campaigns and initiatives.


Feel free to reach out to me via email or connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kianoosh-ashouritaklimi/)!
